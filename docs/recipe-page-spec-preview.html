Homemade Sambar Recipe with Toor Dal and Vegetables

A comforting South Indian lentil stew made with toor dal, tamarind and fresh vegetables, finished with a fragrant tempering. Serve it hot with rice, idli or dosa.

Story & background

Sambar is the dish that turns a plain plate of rice into a proper meal in our home. This is the version I grew up eating: toor dal cooked until creamy, a tangy tamarind base and whatever vegetables the market had that morning.

How to Make Homemade Sambar

1

Pressure Cook the Dal

Rinse the toor dal and pressure cook it with water, turmeric and a little oil for 4 whistles, until it is completely soft.

2

Prepare the Tamarind Juice and Vegetables

Soak the tamarind in warm water for 10 minutes, then squeeze out the juice. Chop the vegetables into evenly sized pieces so they cook at the same pace.

3

Add Vegetables and Cook the Sambar Optional: extra vegetables

Boil the tamarind juice with the tomatoes and sambar powder for 8 minutes. Add the vegetables and simmer until tender, then stir in the mashed dal.

4

Prepare the Tempering

Heat the ghee, splutter the mustard seeds, then add the dried chillies and curry leaves. Pour the tempering over the sambar and cover for 2 minutes.

Tip

Sambar thickens as it rests. Add a splash of hot water when reheating.
