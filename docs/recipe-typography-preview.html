Sweet Pongal with Jaggery (Sakkarai Pongal)

A temple-style sweet made with rice, moong dal and jaggery, finished with ghee-roasted cashews and cardamom.

Story & background

Sakkarai Pongal is cooked on Thai Pongal day and offered to the Sun God first. The slow-cooked rice and dal turn soft and creamy, and the jaggery syrup gives it a deep caramel flavour.

Ingredients

Servings: 4   0.5x · 1x · 2x
1 cup raw rice
1/4 cup moong dal
1 1/2 cups powdered jaggery
3 tbsp ghee
10 cashews
1/2 tsp cardamom powder

How to make Sweet Pongal with Jaggery

1

Roast and cook the dal and rice

Dry roast the moong dal until it smells nutty. Rinse it with the rice, then pressure cook with 4 cups of water for 4 whistles until the mixture is soft and mushy.

2

Make the jaggery syrup

Melt the jaggery with 1/2 cup of water, then strain to remove impurities. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes until it thickens slightly. Do not let it reach a string consistency.

3

Combine and finish

Mash the rice and dal lightly, add the syrup and cook on low heat for 5 minutes. Fry the cashews in ghee, then add them with the cardamom and the remaining ghee.
